- Patient First Name
- Patient Last Name
- Patient Date of Birth (MM/DD/YYYY)

- Email
- Phone (123-456-7890)

- Insurance Provider (dropdown)

- Upload Front of Insurance Card (Image Only, Max 15MB)
- Upload Back of Insurance Card (Image Only, Max 15MB)

- Were you referred here? If so, by who? (textarea)
